A crate with nothing to buy has its Shopping list button greyed out (it looked clickable), and a row with no priced bundle says why once instead of twice.
- Merchant's Favor: below level 10 the empty list says crates open at level 10 and how to see them anyway. The ranking updates after you open a profession window (a new recipe can make a bundle cheaper to craft).
- Crafting list: "Send missing to Auctions" waits for item names the game hasn't loaded yet and tries again by itself, without the "couldn't send the list: loading" message.
- "Buy what the route needs": clicking again after Cancel keeps the price up its full 8 seconds.
- Sorting your bank is lighter: the bank is counted once when things settle, not once per slot.
- Shift-click on a crate or recipe links it in chat more reliably.
- /ecraft > Trainers and merchants: the button reads "Forget this realm's prices" (it was cut short). Merchant's Favor and Trainers and merchants have their own page icons, and the Tooltips page's descriptions are no longer cut off.
- The "Window size" setting is named for what it sizes: the Crafting list, the recipe browser and Merchant's Favor.
- The CraftColors addon is now part of EverlookDB Crafting. In the profession window each recipe name is in its skill-up colour (orange, yellow, green, gray, or your own shades), the row under the mouse brightens, and after the name you see the chance that one craft raises your skill ("37%", on yellow and green recipes) and how many you can make from your bags ("(4)"; "(4 +5)" with your bank and other characters turned on). Where the game already shows "[4]", the count isn't said twice, and a long name is shortened just enough to make room. Settings are on the new "Profession window" page of /ecraft (the search box finds each one); `/ecraft colors off` switches the colours off, `/ecraft reset colors` puts them back.
- Your CraftColors settings are carried over the first time both addons are enabled. An older CraftColors that is still enabled keeps drawing and EverlookDB Crafting stands down, saying once that you can disable CraftColors; the last CraftColors stands down by itself instead.
- Merchant's Favor: "couldn't make the list: loading" is gone. When the game hasn't loaded an item's name yet (a crate you've never had), the list waits a moment and tries again by itself.
- Merchant's Favor: a Shopping list button on every crate puts that crate and its cheapest fill on a shopping list of its own in EverlookDB Auctions ("Favor: Apprentice Textiles"), minus what you already have, so lists for different crates don't replace each other. "Add to list" is now "Craft list".
- Merchant's Favor: each crate's numbers sit on the right: total cost, + Favor, and gold per Favor. On the left: the crate, what to fill it with, and the cost split, crate + fill = total. Sort by "Cheapest per Favor" or "Cheapest total" (the cheapest crate to fill for some Favor now).
- Merchant's Favor (`/ecraft favor`, also a page in /ecraft): every Waylaid Crate ranked by gold per Favor at today's prices. A crate's cost is the crate on the auction house plus its cheapest ONE bundle (each item bought or crafted with a recipe one of your characters knows, whichever is cheaper); the other bundles show on hover. Each row has the Favor, the money and reputation it pays (crate turn-ins give none), the total, a Map button to Marcy Baker or Dokimi and, when the cheapest bundle is crafted, "Add to list". Filters: tier, your level, Alliance or Horde, and "My bags free" (crates and materials you have cost nothing). The one button sends what to buy for the picked crate to EverlookDB Auctions' shopping list "Merchant's Favor". Crates without a known Favor (Artisan) or a price sort last and say why. A Waylaid Crate's tooltip shows "Merchant's Favor 15, ~2s per Favor" and the cheapest fill. Crate data: the game files (bundles), AllTheThings (Favor, MIT), EG Link (money, reputation, CC BY 4.0).

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-20)
- New addon. `/ecraft` (also `/eprof`) opens its window: the Skill-up planner, your characters, prices and the guide's settings, with a search box that finds any setting.
- Crafting list (`/ecraft list`): plan what to craft, account wide. Each recipe has - and + for its count (Shift: by 5) and a Show button; each craft takes one off. Under it a material tree with what you have in bags, bank and on your other characters ("4 in bags, 6 on Bankalt"), shared out so a stack counts once. Materials one of your characters can make (bolts, bars) break down into their own materials, with a Craft or Buy switch on each (right-click works too); transmute-style loops are cut. Vendor goods have a Map button to the nearest vendor. One button, "Send missing to Auctions", saves the rest as the shopping list "Crafting list" in EverlookDB Auctions (vendor goods stay off it). `/ecraft list add <recipe> [count]` and `/ecraft list clear` work from chat.
- Recipe browser (`/ecraft recipes [words]`): every recipe of a profession, learned or not, in its own window. Search by the recipe's name or a reagent's ("silk cloth" lists everything made with it); filters All, Known, Not known and Favourites (right-click a recipe to star it). Each recipe shows its skill colours and your chance now, how many your bags make, every reagent with where it comes from (crafted by which of your characters, vendors with a Map button, gathering, the auction house price), how to learn it (trainer with fee and Map, vendor patterns, Merchant's Favor vendors, the top droppers with Map, quests, reputation; "source unknown" when our data has none) and which of your characters know it. "Add to crafting list" puts it on the list.
- A cleaner look: the windows have a blue top line, row cards with line icons, and one filled button per screen; the guide is blue too. The settings window's search box sits top centre. The Crafting list and recipe browser move and resize on their own and never close another window; one setting sizes both.
- Skill-up planner: the cheapest route from your skill to a target at today's prices, only through recipes you know or can learn now for your faction (the same rule as everlookdb.com's professions guide). Steps with expected crafts and crafts for a 90% buffer, cost tiles (materials, patterns, trainer fees, total; resale shown but not counted), rank trainings with a Map button to the nearest trainer. The route is kept per character until you change the target or a switch, or press Recalculate.
- Skill-up guide: a small movable checklist with the current step's live skill bar and chance, what to buy at the auction house and from vendors (Map to the nearest vendor), what to make first, the materials still needed and every step. Right-click a step to mark it done; Show recipe selects it in your profession window.
- Your materials on every character count (bags, reagent bag, bank and reagent bank from your last visit), unless "Don't count my alts' materials" is on. Names stay on your PC.
- Prices from EverlookDB Auctions or Auctionator; vendor goods at the merchant's price. A material with no price shows "?" and is never guessed in a total.
- The profession scanner never saves a linked or guild profession as yours, and uses the server's reagents where a profession window shows other ones than the game files.
- Item tooltips: on a material, how many of your recipes use it and how many more your skill-up route needs; on something crafted, whether you or your alts on this realm can make it (with your skill, in the recipe's colour); on a recipe, which of your characters already know it, can learn it now, or later. Finer print follows the shared "Tooltip details" setting (always, or while Shift is held). Each part can be turned off in /ecraft > Tooltips.
- Guild crafters: guild members who run EverlookDB Crafting see which recipes each other's current character knows, in recipe and item tooltips ("Guild can make this"). Guild channel only, throttled, names kept in memory only and never saved. Turn it off in /ecraft > Guild.
- Trainer fees and merchant prices you see are remembered per realm and used instead of the data; a learn skill a trainer showed is no longer marked as an estimate.
- "Buy what the route needs": a bar under an open merchant that sells vendor materials your skill-up route still needs. The first click shows what it buys and the price, the second buys it, and never more than the route needs.
- Tools: recipes that need a tool (Blacksmith Hammer, Arclight Spanner, runed rods, Philosopher's Stone, Flint and Tinder) say so on their route step, and the route adds a "Make" or "Buy" step for the tool before the first step that needs it, unless you have one (a Gnomish Army Knife or a better rod counts). Tool data from the game files.
- Data: trainer fees for every rank of First Aid, Cooking, Fishing, Mining, Herbalism and Skinning, and for every trainer recipe, as WoW Forever's trainers ask them (EG Link), else classic-db, else a rank book's price; quest ranks stay "?". Recipes get their Forever learn sources from AllTheThings (MIT): Merchant's Favor patterns with their cost and reputation, drops (who drops it, chance unknown), boss and dungeon drops, and learn skills where the site only estimates one. Routing is unchanged: only recipes you can reliably learn are routed.
